MRTKLIB, mrtklib-docker-ui and mrtklib-quickstart are open-source projects
developed and maintained by **hato.GNSS**.
Support helps the projects work with more receivers and correction services.

Sponsorship through GitHub Sponsors supports all open-source projects
by hato.GNSS, including [snappnt](/oss/snappnt/). The same policy applies.

This page explains how support is accepted, what it is used for,
and how it is disclosed.

## What support is used for · 支援の使い道

- GNSS receivers and antennas, to support and test more hardware
- RF cables, splitters, tripods and other measurement equipment
- A PC for analysis and benchmark runs
- Standards documents needed for decoding (for example, the latest RTCM standard)

Each purchase is recorded in the [ledger](#ledger--使途の記録) below.

## Ways to be involved · 関わり方

### Financial support · 金銭による支援

- Through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/h-shiono).
- In return: thanks and, if you wish, your name on this page. Nothing else.
- Sponsorship does **not** include priority support, priority bug fixes,
  a say in the roadmap, or any influence on evaluation results.
- Funds are received by hato.GNSS.

### Equipment gifts · 機材の寄贈

- Receivers, antennas, cables and similar equipment are welcome as gifts.
- Loans are not accepted.
- In return: thanks and, if you wish, your name on this page. Nothing else.
- The conditions below apply to all gifted equipment.
- To offer equipment, write to [contact@hatognss.dev](mailto:contact@hatognss.dev)
  with the model, the quantity, and any notes.

### Commissioned work · 依頼による業務

- Commissioned articles, evaluations and feature development are paid work,
  not support.
  Each is covered by its own agreement.
  For details, write to [contact@hatognss.dev](mailto:contact@hatognss.dev).
- Every agreement allows the disclosure described below.
- Conditions 2, 3 and 6 below also apply to commissioned evaluations.
- Work is accepted only when it helps MRTKLIB reach more users.
- Funded changes to the projects are released in each project,
  under its license.
- Separate software built on MRTKLIB (for example, an embedded version)
  may be offered under other terms.
  MRTKLIB itself stays open source.

## Conditions for equipment · 機材を受け取る条件

1. **No obligation to publish.** Receiving equipment does not oblige
   the projects to evaluate it or to publish results.
2. **Results are published as they are.** Unfavorable results are not
   changed or withheld at the provider's request.
   No configuration or condition is left out
   because its result was unfavorable.
3. **Review before publication, on request.** If the provider asks,
   the draft is shared before publication.
   Factual errors are corrected.
   No other change is made at the provider's request.
   The provider does not approve the content.
   If the draft was reviewed, the published result says so.
4. **The source is disclosed.** Where the equipment is used in a result
   that makes claims about a product, the source is stated: by name,
   or as "provided by the manufacturer" or "provided by a distributor".
5. **No obligation to support the product.** Receiving equipment does not
   oblige the projects to add or keep support for it.
6. **Firmware and settings are recorded.** Firmware versions and settings
   used in evaluations are published with the results.

## Disclosure policy · 開示の方針

There are two kinds of disclosure, handled differently.
This policy applies to results published from December 2026 onward.

### On each result · 成果物ごとの開示

- Some results make claims about a product: articles, evaluations,
  benchmarks and talks. When such a result uses gifted equipment,
  or is commissioned work, this is stated on that result.
  This applies even if the provider is not on the supporters list.
- Code and how-to documentation are not labelled per feature.
  The exception is hardware pages in the MRTKLIB documentation:
  they note when the device was a gift, by name or as
  "provided by the manufacturer" or "provided by a distributor".
  Release notes do not carry this information.
- hato.GNSS has paid-work or equipment relationships with some
  GNSS hardware vendors. This includes funding for the development
  of some features. Where such a relationship is relevant
  to a published result, it is stated on that result.

### Supporters list (opt-in) · 支援者一覧（希望者のみ）

- Individuals and companies are listed **only if they wish**.
- Public sponsors on GitHub are listed here.
  Private sponsors are not.
- Listing is a thank-you. It does not mean that the projects
  endorse a product, or that a supporter endorses the projects.
